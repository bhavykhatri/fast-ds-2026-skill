# CBDT FAST-DS FAQs — summaries (Q1–93)

Paraphrased summaries for quick reference. Always quote and link the official text when advising:
- Q1–50: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/81799/15520974/FAST-DS-FAQs.pdf
- Q51–93 (Supplementary, 20-Nov-2026): https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/supplementary-faqs-fast-ds-pdf

FAQs are the department's stated position, not law. Where an FAQ and the statute/Rules differ, the statute and Rules prevail — flag it to the user.

## A. About the scheme (Q1–5)
- **Q1** One-time voluntary scheme in Finance Act 2026 ss.130–144 to declare foreign assets/income for a specified tax or fee.
- **Q2** In force from 16-Aug-2026. **Q3** Last date 31-Dec-2026. **Q4** Valuation date 31-Mar-2026.
- **Q5** Authority: Pr. DGIT / DGIT (Systems); fully online.

## B. Eligibility (Q6–11)
- **Q6–8** Residents; and NRs/RNORs who were resident in the year the income relates to or the asset was acquired. RNOR status is stated per item in Form 1.
- **Q9** Grounds: no return; omitted from a return filed before commencement; or escaped assessment.
- **Q10** File between 16-Aug-2026 and 31-Dec-2026. **Q11** Any previous year, subject to caps.

## C. Scope (Q12–16)
- **Q12** Two categories: Sl.1 undisclosed asset/income; Sl.2 asset already taxed or acquired as NR but not in the Schedule.
- **Q13** Undisclosed asset = no or unsatisfactory explanation of the source. **Q14** Undisclosed foreign income = chargeable in India but not offered.
- **Q15** Sl.1 cap: asset value (31-3-2026) + income ≤ ₹1 crore. **Q16** Sl.2 cap: ≤ ₹5 crore.

## D. Amount payable (Q17–20)
- **Q17** Sl.1: 30% tax + an equal amount. **Q18** Example: account ₹60L + income ₹20L → ₹24L + ₹24L = ₹48L.
- **Q19** Sl.2: ₹1 lakh flat. **Q20** Over ₹5 crore → not eligible.

## E. Valuation (Q21–36)
- **Q21** Higher of cost and open-market value on 31-3-2026 (valuer report); else indexed cost.
- **Q22–23** Bullion/jewellery/art: same rule. **Q24** Quoted shares: higher of cost and average of the day's low and high on 31-3-2026. **Q25** Not traded that day → nearest earlier trading day.
- **Q26** Unquoted equity: book-value formula. **Q27** Other unquoted securities: valuer, else indexed cost. **Q28** Immovable property: valuer in that country, else indexed cost.
- **Q29–31** Bank account: sum of deposits since opening, excluding re-deposits of withdrawals; if declared under BMA Ch. VI, only later deposits. Worked tables in the FAQ.
- **Q32** Firm/AOP/LLP interest: net assets by capital, residue by agreement/profit ratio. **Q33** Residuary assets: higher of cost and arm's-length price, else indexed cost.
- **Q34** Reinvested proceeds: reduce the old asset's/account's value by the amount reinvested.
- **Q35** Report in INR; RBI reference rate on the valuation date (non-designated currencies via USD).
- **Q36** For assets other than bank accounts, a variance up to 20% doesn't by itself void the declaration (Rule 5(2)).

## F. Filing (Q37–39)
- **Q37** Form 1, electronically. **Q38** Many assets/incomes in one Form 1 — repeat the annexure entries.
- **Q39** Upload proof of acquisition/income, and a valuation report where valuation is carried out.

## G. Orders and payment (Q40–46)
- **Q40** Form 2 within 1 month from the end of the month of Form 1.
- **Q41** Pay within 2 months from the end of the month Form 2 is received. **Q42–43** Up to 2 further months at 1% simple interest per month or part. **Q44** Outer limit: 4 months from the end of the month of Form 2; then the benefit is lost.
- **Q45** Report payment in Form 3 with proof. **Q46** Form 4 certifies payment within 1 month from the end of the month Form 3 is received.

## H. Effect (Q47–49)
- **Q47** No rectification, revision, set-off or relief on assessments already made.
- **Q48** Immunity from further tax, penalty and prosecution under the BMA; declared amounts not included in total income under the IT Act or BMA.
- **Q49** Pending assessments: the AO must take the declaration into account.

## I. Exclusions (Q50)
- **Q50** PMLA proceeds of crime with proceedings started/pending; AYs with a completed BMA assessment.

## Supplementary FAQs (20-Nov-2026)

### J. Declarations (Q51–55)
- **Q51** Category 1: declare only income/assets not offered to tax. Income already offered needn't be declared.
- **Q52** Category 2: declare only assets missing from Schedule FA. Assets already reported needn't be declared.
- **Q53–54** Immunity covers only what is declared (and paid on time). Anything later found undeclared gets no immunity.
- **Q55** USD→INR for valuation: no RBI rate on 31-3-2026, so the last available rate, **₹94.6543 (30-3-2026)**, applies.

### K. Joint accounts (Q56–61)
- **Q56** Each joint holder reports separately under BMA s.42/43.
- **Q57** A named joint holder (e.g. a spouse) must declare even if the other holder funded and disclosed it, and can use the scheme.
- **Q58** Value joint assets by definite shares (₹30L at 3:3:4 → ₹9L/₹9L/₹12L), or equally if none (₹10L each). Contributions are ignored.
- **Q59** The ₹5 crore cap is tested on each declarant's own Form 1.
- **Q60–61** Directors/employees/signatories required to report a company's foreign account under BMA s.42/43 can use Category 2; the account is valued under Rule 3(1)(e).

### L. Residency and eligibility (Q62–66)
- **Q62** A person now non-resident who opened an account while resident is eligible.
- **Q63** Assets acquired while RNOR and not reported after becoming ROR → Category 2; select the RNOR option in Form 1 Part B.
- **Q64** A concluded BMA assessment bars that asset even if under appeal; pending proceedings don't.
- **Q65** A concluded BMA penalty proceeding bars that asset; an asset penalised under s.42/43 can't go in Category 2.
- **Q66** Cost comparison: BMA 30% tax + 90% penalty (120%) vs FAST-DS 60%; BMA ₹10L penalty per asset vs FAST-DS ₹1L flat for all assets.

### M. Sources and categories (Q67–73)
- **Q67** Accounts with an aggregate balance ≤ ₹20 lakh at all times in the year are exempt from the ₹10L penalty and prosecution; if funded from disclosed sources, no declaration is needed. Undisclosed deposits must still be declared.
- **Q68** Returning NR whose foreign account was funded from post-tax income: not undisclosed. Interest earned while NR isn't taxable; for FY 2025-26, a revised return by 31-3-2027 with the interest and Schedule FA is sufficient BMA compliance.
- **Q69** An asset declared in Category 1 needn't be repeated in Category 2.
- **Q70** Any undisclosed income embedded in an asset → Category 2 isn't available; Category 1 if eligible.
- **Q71** Both categories can be combined in one Form 1.
- **Q72** No double taxation where undisclosed income bought an asset — the income is reduced by the amount invested (Rule 3(3)).
- **Q73** Fractional shares: exact fraction × average of the day's low and high on 31-3-2026, converted at the RBI rate; Schedule A5 accepts the decimal quantity.

### N. Confidentiality (Q74–75)
- **Q74** No human interface; scrutiny is separate and risk-based; declared investments aren't added to income in any year.
- **Q75** Declarations are confidential like an ITR (s.138 IT Act via BMA).

### O. 20% tolerance (Q76–79)
- **Q76** Applies where a recognised valuer's report is used. ₹60L declared → safe up to ₹72L (AO's ₹70L: fine; ₹78L: not, goes to s.141).
- **Q77** Not for bank accounts. **Q78** Yes for immovable property valued by report.
- **Q79** If indexed cost is used (no valuation), it's final; it can't be replaced later and the tolerance doesn't apply. Example: $28,000 bought FY 2018-19 → $28,000 × 380/280 = $38,000.

### P. Valuation and miscellaneous (Q80–93)
- **Q80** Gifted asset: donee's cost = donor's cost; funded from disclosed LRS money → Category 2. Example indexed cost $40,000 × 380/272 = $55,882 → ₹52,89,472 at 94.6543.
- **Q81** An unsecured loan to a non-resident is a foreign financial asset and can be declared.
- **Q82–83** One ₹1 lakh fee covers all Category 2 assets across all years, within ₹5 crore.
- **Q84** One consolidated Form 1. It can be refiled before Form 2 is issued (latest counts); **after Form 2, no further Form 1**, even for omissions.
- **Q85** A rejected Form 1 can be filed afresh. **Q86** A wrong Form 1 can be replaced, but not after Form 2.
- **Q87** **No DTAA or foreign tax credit** against the Category 1 amount.
- **Q88** Assets found in a search/survey can be declared unless a BMA assessment is completed or PMLA prosecution has started.
- **Q89** Pending assessment with a time-bar: the declaration stays valid; give Form 2 to the AO and pay early; after Form 4, the AO can rectify any addition.
- **Q90** HUFs, firms, companies, LLPs, AOPs and trusts are eligible; a legal heir can declare for a deceased person (s.159).
- **Q91** Form 2 is system-generated; raise a grievance for apparent errors, but the payment deadline isn't extended.
- **Q92** Assets bought before 1-4-2001: base = higher of cost and FMV on 1-4-2001, indexed from CII 100 to 380, in foreign currency, then converted.
- **Q93** An asset held across several years is reported **once**, in the earliest relevant previous year of acquisition, at FMV on 31-3-2026.
